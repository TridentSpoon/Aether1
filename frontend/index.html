                    📓 Memory
                    what it remembers, and where that lives
                
                
          
                    
          
                        
                        
                        
          
                            
                            
                            
                        
          
                        
          
                            A folder of markdown notes — what the AI remembers about you. Open it in
                            any editor or in Obsidian, where the [[links]]
                            between notes draw themselves as a graph. Edit or delete anything; it is
                            your folder.
                        
          
                        
          
                            
                            
                        
          
                        
          
                            Every exchange is added word for word to a note named after
                            today's date, inside daily in the folder
                            above — nothing is shortened. A day long enough to outgrow one note
                            carries on in the next one. No approval is
                            asked, because this only ever writes into its own folder. Switch it off and
                            conversations stay in the app and nowhere else. Everything else — what
                            it decides to remember about you — is still only written with
                            your approval.

                    🌐 Network
                    whether anything may leave this machine
                
                
          
                    
          
                        
          
                            
                            
                        
          
                        
          
                            Nothing leaves this machine or your local network. Cloud providers are
                            refused, speech uses Piper or stays silent rather than sending your words
                            to Microsoft, and update checks and model downloads stop. A model server
                            on your own LAN still works — that is the point. Turning this on
                            does not delete anything; switch it off and your cloud settings are still
                            there.

                    ⚡ Startup & Performance
                    what runs on login, and how to quiet it back down
                
                
          
                    
          
                        
          
                            
                            
                        
          
                        
          
                            Starts resident in the tray as soon as you log in, exactly like
                            opening it by hand. Turning this off removes the login entry
                            — it does not touch anything already running.
                        
          
                    
          

                    
          
                        
          
                            
                            
                        
          
                        
          
                            When AETHER1 starts, also start Ollama if it isn't already
                            running — the same thing the Quick Commands "Start Server"
                            button does, just without you having to click it. Does nothing
                            if Ollama isn't installed, or if something is already listening.
                        
          
                    
          

                    
          
                        
          
                            
                            
                        
          
                        
          
                            On launch, AETHER1 checks that speech really plays — not
                            just that synthesis ran without error, but that audio was
                            actually decoded and produced sound. On, you'll hear a short
                            phrase confirming it. Off, the same check runs silently and
                            only speaks up if it fails.
