# Third-party notices

A1 includes open-source software and assets from other projects. The following notices apply
to files embedded in the application and to the offline installers. Each component keeps its
own license; A1's GPL-3.0-only license does not replace these component terms.

## Text-to-speech

### Piper

- Project: https://github.com/rhasspy/piper
- License: MIT
- Piper is not bundled or installed by A1; the app can invoke a user-installed Piper binary
  with a model the user supplies. Voice models have individual training-dataset terms. A1's
  built-in voice downloader is disabled until those terms are reviewed. The former Lessac
  default is not bundled because its dataset terms are non-commercial. Users may configure
  models they have rights to use.
- Piper's engine license does not cover its voice datasets or weights. Do not treat an
  upstream model card's code/model license label as proof that the training data is clear.

### eSpeak NG

- Project: https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng
- License: GPL-3.0-or-later
- Installed through the Linux distribution package manager; not bundled by A1.

## Speech recognition

### whisper.cpp

- Project: https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp
- License: MIT
- Offline installers include a statically built `whisper-cli` executable.

### Whisper `ggml-small.bin` model

- Source: https://huggingface.co/ggerganov/whisper.cpp
- The file is a conversion of OpenAI Whisper model weights.
- License: MIT, https://github.com/openai/whisper/blob/main/LICENSE
